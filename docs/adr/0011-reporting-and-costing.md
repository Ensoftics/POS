# ADR 0011 — Reporting, moving-average cost and exports

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-17)

## Context

Owners need to know what they sold, what tax they owe, what they actually earned and what
their shelves are worth. Profit needs the cost of each item at the moment it was sold,
which the system did not record until now.

## Decision

### Moving weighted-average cost, captured when goods move

- `variant_costs` keeps one average cost per variant across all branches, with four
  decimals of a minor unit, so many small receipts do not drift.
- Each goods receipt updates it under a row lock:
  `new = (on_hand × average + received × unit_cost) / (on_hand + received)`. Negative stock
  counts as none, so goods oversold before their cost was known do not distort the average.
- Every `SALE` stock movement records its **cost of goods** (`cost_minor`) at the average
  at that moment; when there is no average yet, the variant's manual cost price is used; with
  neither, the cost stays unknown and reports say how many units that affects.
- Restocked returns come back at the cost they left with (that sale's own SALE movements,
  pro-rated), so a return never changes historic profit.
- Existing data was backfilled once: the average was set from all past receipts, and past
  sales and returns were costed at it. That is an approximation for history only.

### Reports read immutable records

Sales, tax, profit, stock valuation, receivables and the dashboard are SQL aggregations over
sales, sale lines, payments, returns, stock movements and customer ledger entries — never
over mutable balances, except current stock and customer balances for "as of now" reports.

- Periods are **business days in the tenant's timezone** (inclusive); the default is the
  last seven days. Refunds are reported on the day of the return, not the day of the sale.
- Sales: by day, branch, cashier, category, item or payment method (cash net of change).
- Tax: taxable amount and tax per rate, minus tax refunded on returns.
- Profit: revenue excluding tax and net of returns, against cost of goods; margin in basis
  points.
- Receivables: each owing customer's balance aged 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days, with
  payments settling the oldest charges first.
- Shift (Z) report: the shift's own summary, counted cash, difference and cash movements.
- Grouping expressions come from a closed list in code; parameters are bound — no SQL is
  built from input.

### Access

`reports.view` (owners, managers) for sales, tax, receivables and the dashboard;
`reports.profit` (owners, managers) for anything showing cost: profit and stock value.
Cashiers see none of it.

### Exports

- **CSV** is generated by the API with the same rows (`format=csv`): amounts as plain
  decimals, a total row, and cells starting with `= + - @` prefixed so a product name cannot
  run as a spreadsheet formula.
- **PDF** is produced by printing the report's print layout from the browser (Print → Save as
  PDF). The API does not render PDF files; a server-side renderer can be added later if
  scheduled or e-mailed reports need it.

## Consequences

- Profit is exact from now on and explains itself: each sale's cost is stored, not recomputed.
- Changing a receipt's cost after the fact is not possible (receipts are immutable), so cost
  corrections go through new receipts or adjustments.
- Reports on very large tenants may need materialised daily summaries later; the queries are
  indexed by tenant and time today.
